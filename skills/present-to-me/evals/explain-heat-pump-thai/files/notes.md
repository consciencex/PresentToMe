# Hot water for the building: notes

Riverside Residence, 180 units, 22 floors. I am the building manager. I present to the juristic committee (7 owners, none are engineers) on Saturday. They must agree before I can ask the owners' meeting for the money.

## Today
- Central hot water from 2 electric boilers, 45 kW each, on the roof.
- The boilers are 14 years old. The maker says 15 years of service life. One failed for 3 days in March.
- Hot water used 310,000 kWh of electricity last year (sub-meter).
- We pay 4.2 baht a kWh.
- Boiler maintenance: 35,000 baht a year.

## The proposal
- Replace both boilers with an air-source heat pump water heater on the roof.
- Supplier datasheet: COP 3.5. That means 1 kWh of electricity moves 3.5 kWh of heat into the water.
- Quote: 2,400,000 baht installed, including a 3,000 litre tank.
- Maintenance: 60,000 baht a year.
- Needs 6 x 3 m on the roof. We have it.
- Noise 58 dB at 1 m. The nearest unit is two floors below.
- It blows out cool air. The supplier can duct it to the lift machine room.

## The committee's rule
- Equipment must pay back in under 5 years.

## What they do not understand
- How can it give more heat than the electricity it uses? Last time someone said "it sounds like a scam".
