# Notes for the board: laptops for grades 6 to 8

Riverside Middle School. I am the principal. The board votes on the 2027-28 budget line next week. Five members. The treasurer is the sceptic: two years ago the tablet programme was abandoned after one year.

## Today
- 540 students in grades 6 to 8.
- 4 carts of 30 shared laptops (120 laptops). The carts are booked 92% of periods.
- Teachers report losing about 8 minutes of a 50-minute class moving the cart, handing out and logging in (survey of 22 teachers, March).
- State tests move online from spring 2028.
- The carts are due for replacement anyway: $41,000 every 4 years.

## The pilot
- One grade 6 class, 28 students, 9 weeks this spring, one laptop each.
- Homework completed on time: 71% before, 84% during (teacher's records).
- 2 laptops needed repair in 9 weeks.

## Quotes
- Buy: $289 a laptop, expected life 4 years. Management licence $18 a laptop a year. Repairs: assume 6% of the fleet a year at $120 each.
- Lease: $96 a laptop a year for 4 years, repairs and licence included.
- Either way: 40 hotspots for students with no home internet (12% of students told us they have none), $15 a month each, 10 months a year.

## What I want
Approve the programme for all 540 students from August 2027. I think buying is better than leasing but I am not sure the numbers say so.

## What they will ask
- What about breakage?
- Is this more screen time?
- What happened to the tablets?
