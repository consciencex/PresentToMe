Three tests

Before we sign anything, the roof has to pass three tests. Is there room, is there sun, and does it pay back inside our limit?

Roof. The panels need well under the roof area we have, and the structure carries their weight with room to spare. [If asked: the spare load and the panel weight are on the box.]

Sun. The line marks the sun hours a day we need for the numbers to work. The site averages more than that across the year.

Payback. The line is our rule for any equipment. Buying the panels pays back well inside it. [This box reads the money model, so it moves if anyone changes the inputs.] Next: what one sunny day looks like.
