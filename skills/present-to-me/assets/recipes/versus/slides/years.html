Example data. Lines computed by the deck model. Like for like, with the same heavy work on both laptops and the time both spend on it left out. The value of an hour is the buyer's own estimate.

The cost over the years

Now money. This chart counts two things for each laptop. Its price, and the extra hours you wait for heavy work, at what you say an hour is worth. The orange line is the Light 13. It starts at its price and climbs, because every week you wait a little longer.

The blue line is the Studio 15. It starts higher, at its price, and then stays flat. Where the two lines cross, the Studio 15 has paid back the difference.

Before the crossing the Light 13 has cost less. After it the Studio 15 has, for as long as you keep it.
