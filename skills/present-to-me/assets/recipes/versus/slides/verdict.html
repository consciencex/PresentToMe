Who should take which

So, who should take which. If you are out most days, or heavy work is a small part of your week, take the Light 13. It is lighter, it runs longer and it costs less.

If heavy work at a desk is part of every week, take the Studio 15. It gives you that time back, and with enough of it the higher price pays for itself. The frame marks the one our numbers picked a moment ago.
