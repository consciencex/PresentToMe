    Why they differ

    Open the Studio 15 and the reason is inside. From the outside the two look alike. Inside, three parts are not the same.

    The chip. It draws far more power than the chip in the Light 13, and the card has both figures. Power is speed, so the heavy job finishes sooner.

    The fan. All that power becomes heat. The fan carries it out, so the chip holds its speed for the whole job. The Light 13 has no fan. It is silent, and it has to hold back.

    The battery. It is much larger, to feed that chip, and it is most of the extra weight. Even so it runs fewer hours. One more thing to remember. On battery the chip is held back to save charge, so the speed is there only at a desk. Next, what that speed is worth over the years you keep it.
