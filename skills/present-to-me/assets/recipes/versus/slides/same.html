What is the same

Start with what is the same, because it is most of the laptop. Each line joins the two where they match. Memory, storage, the display, the ports, the camera and the warranty are identical.

So set all of these aside. A review that compares them will not help you choose. What is left is a short list, and it is where the two part ways. Next, those measures one at a time.
