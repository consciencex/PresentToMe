What is different

These are the measures that separate them. I will take them one at a time, and the ring marks the better value on each row.

Weight. The Light 13 is the lighter one. You feel that difference on every day it is in your bag.

Battery. The Light 13 runs longer on one charge. That is a working day and the evening after it.

Price. The Light 13 costs less. Three rows so far, and all three go to the Light 13.

Screen. Here the Studio 15 is ahead. If you work in two windows side by side, you notice it.
