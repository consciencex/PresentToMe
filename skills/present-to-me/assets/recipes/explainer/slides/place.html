01 What it is

It stands between the roof and the rooms.

    Noon on the roof. Evening in the rooms. The battery joins the two.

    Example data. Example Home is invented. The roof's best hour and the evening's use come from the deck model.

    Where it goes

    The cabinet goes here, on the ground, by the wall where the power comes in. Next to the house it is small, and it takes less floor than a doormat. It works with two other things.

    The first is the roof. The panels up there make power whenever there is daylight, and most of it around noon.

    The second is the rooms. They use a little all day and a lot after dark. So the roof peaks at noon and the rooms peak in the evening, and the battery stands between the two. Next, one day, hour by hour.
