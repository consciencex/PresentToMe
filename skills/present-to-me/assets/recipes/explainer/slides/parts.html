01 What it is

Four parts, and only one stores energy.

    The cells hold the energy. The other three keep them safe and in step.

    Example data. Weight, capacity and power are typical values for a cabinet this size, from an invented data sheet. Capacity and power follow the deck model.

    Four parts

    Here is the cabinet with nothing hidden. It is no bigger than a suitcase, and it comes apart into four layers, from the floor up.

    At the bottom, the base. A steel foot, bolted down. It is there because the cabinet is heavy, about as heavy as a full fridge.

    Above it, the cells. This block is the battery itself. Every unit of energy the cabinet holds is in here, and the number on the card says how much.

    Then the control board. It decides when to charge and when to give power back. It also sets how much can flow at one moment. That limit comes back later.

    And the cover. Sealed steel, no fan, nothing that moves. So four parts, and only the cells store anything. Next, where does this cabinet go in a house?
