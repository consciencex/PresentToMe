Example data. One clear day at an invented house with a 5 kW roof, by the hour, from the deck model.

One day

One clear day, from six in the morning to midnight, in three rows. The roof on top, the battery in the middle, the house below. The grey outlines are the problem. The roof peaks at noon and the house peaks in the evening. Now follow the dot. It is one parcel of the sun's energy.

Step one, up to noon. The roof makes more than the house uses. The spare goes down into the battery, and the battery is full by the time on the card.

Step two, the afternoon. Nothing happens, and that is the point. The battery holds what it has, and the roof still runs the house directly.

Step three, the evening. The sun is gone and the house is at its busiest. The parcel moves down into the house. Orange in the bottom row is our own energy. Blue is the grid, and after sunset there is almost none. Next, the numbers behind this picture.
