One charge is more than one evening.

1 · Size

The evening fits inside

One evening takes less than it holds. There is room to spare.

2 · Time

Hours on one charge

The evening is hours long. A full battery outlasts it.

3 · Share

More of the day is ours

The roof alone covers the daytime. The battery adds the evening.

Example data. Capacity from an invented data sheet. Evening use and the two shares come from the deck model for one clear day.

The numbers

Three numbers make that picture real.

First, size. The tank is filled to what one evening takes in this house. The line above it is what the battery holds. The evening fits, with room to spare.

Second, time. At the evening's average draw, a full battery runs the house for the hours on the card. The evening itself is shorter, as the line under it says.

Third, the share of a day's power that is our own. With the roof alone it is the left bar, because the sun and the evening miss each other. With the battery it is the right bar, about twice as much. [If asked, both bars are the same day, with and without the battery.] So far it sounds perfect. Next, where it stops.
